Governing safety reference

KSHSAA WBGT policy · April 2026

The app applies the published Kansas ranges to each hourly NWS WBGT forecast as a conservative reference for youth football. A local youth-league, district or insurance rule may be stricter and should be followed.

Decision chain

Forecast plans. Field meter decides.

  1. Use the NWS direct-sun WBGT grid for advance planning.
  2. At the activity site, take WBGT readings 30–60 minutes before activity and average 15–20 minutes.
  3. Use the field result for the day; remeasure after an obvious weather change.
